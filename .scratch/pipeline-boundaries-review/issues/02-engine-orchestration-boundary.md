## Question

O pacote `engine` (`pipeline.py`, `quality_gates.py`, `strategies/*`) produz resultados de cálculo consumidos por `orchestration` (`run.py`, `state.py`, `summary.py`) e indiretamente por `rom` (via `orchestration`). Rastreie a fronteira: qual é o shape exato que `engine` promete na saída (tipos, campos opcionais vs. obrigatórios, unidades — percentual vs. fração, decimal vs. float), o que `orchestration` assume dessa saída sem revalidar, e onde uma mudança de shape/precisão/arredondamento no `engine` atravessaria silenciosamente até `orchestration`/`rom` sem erro. Cheque também o outro sentido: o que `orchestration` passa para `engine` (dataset paths, configs de indicador) e se há suposição não garantida aí. Não repita achados de qualidade interna já cobertos em `.scratch/production-readiness-review/issues/02-engine-package-review.md` e `06-orchestration-package-review.md` — foque só na travessia.

Aplique o skill `python-production-engineer` (ler `.agents/skills/python-production-engineer/SKILL.md` por inteiro) para julgar severidade. Achados citam `file:line`.

## Answer

Read every file in `src/pyauditor/engine/` and `src/pyauditor/orchestration/` in full, plus `src/pyauditor/rom/summary.py`, `src/pyauditor/cli/measure.py`, `src/pyauditor/cli/report.py`, `src/pyauditor/cli/dependencies.py`, and `src/pyauditor/config/manifest.py` (the module that actually mediates one of the two crossings below).

### Premise check: `orchestration` never imports `engine`

`grep -rn "pyauditor.engine" src/pyauditor/orchestration/` returns nothing. The engine→orchestration edge the ticket asks about does not exist as a direct import; it's fully mediated by `cli/measure.py` (`run_measure`, imported at `orchestration/run.py:24`) and, for `report`'s dependency check, `cli/report.py`. This matters for the "shape/precision/rounding silently crossing" half of the question:

- `engine.strategies.base.CalculationResult` (`src/pyauditor/engine/strategies/base.py:9-14`) — `result_pct: float` (always the 0–100 percent scale by convention, per-shape: `ratio.py:35` `safe_pct(...)*100`, `segmented_ratio.py:56`, `count_difference.py:30`, but `external_catalog_sum.py:47` hardcodes `result_pct=0.0` as a "no percentage meta for this shape" placeholder — same field, two different meanings depending on shape, not distinguished by type) and `memoria: dict[str, object]` (fully untyped, shape-specific keys: `{"numerator","denominator"}` for `ratio`, `{"categories":[...]}` for `segmented_ratio`/`precomputed_table`, `{"QRC","QCSI","CNI"}` for `count_difference`, `{"occurrences","total_points"}` for `external_catalog_sum`) — **never reaches `orchestration`**. It's consumed and normalized once, at `rom/summary.py:83-105`'s `summarize()`, into `IndicatorSummary` (`rom/summary.py:19-56`), a `frozen` dataclass whose `__post_init__` runtime-validates every field's type (`_require_str`/`_require_numeric`/`_require_bool`, `rom/summary.py:41-53`) before it's ever serialized to the `<id>.json` sidecar (`cli/measure.py:156-159`).
- What `orchestration` actually sees of a `measure` run is `cli/measure.py`'s own `MeasureResult`/`IndicatorOutcome` (`cli/measure.py:31-46`): `status: Status` (`Literal["done","error"]`), and per indicator only `contractual_id: str`, `rom_path`/`summary_path: Path`, `hard_failure: bool`, `error: str | None`. `orchestration/summary.py:61-66`'s `_measure_artifact` reads only `contractual_id`/`hard_failure`. No `result_pct`, no `memoria`, no penalty points, no Decimal/float distinction ever crosses into `orchestration` — so a shape/rounding change inside `engine` cannot silently corrupt anything `orchestration` does; the boolean `hard_failure` it does see is derived purely from quality-gate row counts (`engine/pipeline.py:46-52`, `len(accepted)==0 and len(rejected)>0`), orthogonal to any calculation-shape change. **This part of the ticket's premise doesn't hold for this specific pair of packages** — the real percent/fraction/Decimal drift risk lives at the engine→rom crossing (ticket `04-rom-excel-boundary.md`'s territory), not engine→orchestration.

### What `orchestration` passes into engine-adjacent code, unguarded

The one place `orchestration` does reach past its `cli/*` adapter and touch engine-adjacent config-loading itself, rather than delegating to the already-hardened `run_measure`:

- **`src/pyauditor/orchestration/run.py:160-163`** (`_dispatch`, `command == "measure"` branch) — **HIGH / Resiliência e comportamento sob falha** — `manifest = load_manifest(manifest_path) if manifest_path.exists() else None` calls `config.manifest.load_manifest` (`src/pyauditor/config/manifest.py:96-105`) directly and unguarded. `load_manifest` raises bare `ValueError` (malformed YAML, wrong top-level shape, invalid `DatasetEntry`) or `FileNotFoundError` (TOCTOU: the `.exists()` check at `run.py:162` and the actual `path.read_text()` inside `_load_raw` — `config/manifest.py:74` — are not atomic). Every other place this codebase reaches into engine/config territory wraps the call: `cli/measure.py:101-104` catches `(OSError, ValueError)` around `discover_config_files`, `cli/report.py:111-115` catches the same around `discover_configs` and downgrades it to a warning. This one call site has no such guard, and nothing upstream of it does either — `execute_run`'s dispatch call, `src/pyauditor/orchestration/run.py:286` (`result = _dispatch(command, orgao, request)`), has no try/except around it anywhere in the `while True:` loop (`run.py:264-307`). A malformed `datasets.yaml` therefore doesn't produce an `error` `CommandStateEntry`, doesn't go through `record_failure_and_decide`'s retry/skip/abort flow (`run.py:224-248`), doesn't reach `render_summary`/`exit_code_for_run` — it's a raw, uncaught traceback that kills the whole `pyauditor run` process outright, defeating the state machine this package exists to provide. It also leaves the `running` `CommandStateEntry` just saved at `run.py:279-284` (immediately before the crashing `_dispatch` call) stuck at `status="running"` in the persisted JSON for this invocation; it self-heals only on the *next* invocation via `reset_stale_running` (`state.py:88-100`), not this one.
  Additionally, `dependency_missing` (`run.py:135-152`) — whose entire job is to catch problems *before* the state machine transitions to `running` and dispatches — is blind to this failure mode for `measure`: it routes to `CHECKERS["measure"]` = `check_measure_ready` (`cli/measure.py:50-53`), which unconditionally returns `satisfied=True` and never looks at `datasets.yaml`. So the one pre-dispatch safety net this orchestrator has for exactly this purpose doesn't cover the one unguarded call it makes.
  Fix: either move manifest loading inside `run_measure` (which already owns the `(OSError, ValueError)` handling for `discover_config_files` immediately below it) so it shares that same guard and turns into a normal `MeasureResult(status="error", ...)`, or wrap the `_dispatch` measure branch's `load_manifest` call with the same `except (OSError, ValueError)` pattern used everywhere else at this boundary.

- **`src/pyauditor/config/manifest.py:96`** (`@lru_cache(maxsize=1)` on `load_manifest`), reached via **`src/pyauditor/orchestration/run.py:162`** — **MEDIUM / Corretude** — `execute_run`'s retry loop (`run.py:264-276`, `while True: ... if decision == "retry": continue`) re-invokes `_dispatch` — and therefore `load_manifest(manifest_path)` — on every retry attempt for the same `(command, orgao)`. The retry contract's premise is "re-check current filesystem state and try again" (that's the whole point of re-running `dependency_missing` and `_dispatch` rather than replaying a cached result). But `load_manifest` is cached by `path` alone, for the lifetime of the process, with no mtime/content invalidation. A real, foreseeable interactive-flow scenario: a `measure` command fails because `datasets.yaml` has a wrong delimiter/alias, the operator edits the file and picks "retry" (exactly the affordance `on_failure` returning `"retry"` exists for), and the retry silently reuses the first attempt's now-stale, already-cached `DatasetManifest` — no error, no warning, just the old dataset definitions applied again. This is a correctness/silent-staleness risk, not a crash, and specific to `orchestration`'s retry semantics — `cli/main.py:275-277`'s single-shot, non-retrying call site for the same `load_manifest` doesn't have this exposure, since it's a fresh process per invocation.
  Fix: since `measure()`/`_resolve_source` never re-reads a cached manifest for performance reasons that matter (it's one small YAML parse per command invocation, not a hot loop), either drop `@lru_cache` from `load_manifest`, or key/invalidate it on the file's mtime/content hash rather than path alone, or have `_dispatch` call `load_manifest.cache_clear()` before a retry.

### Reverse direction: config/dataset paths orchestration hands to engine-adjacent code

- `src/pyauditor/orchestration/run.py:159-162` builds `per_orgao_config_dir = request.config_dir / (orgao or "")` and `manifest_path = per_orgao_config_dir / "datasets.yaml"` — a directory-layout convention (`<config_dir>/<orgao>/datasets.yaml`) that `orchestration` assumes without any validation of its own. If `config.scope.orgao` inside a YAML under that directory doesn't match `orgao`, `discover_config_files` (`engine/pipeline.py:134-138`) raises `ValueError`, and that path *is* caught by `run_measure` (`cli/measure.py:101-104`) — so this particular mismatch is safely handled. No further gap found here beyond the manifest-loading issue above; `data_dir`, `output_dir`, `capa_path` are all plain `Path` values engine/`cli/measure.py` validate or handle missing (`capa_path.exists()` check, `cli/measure.py:120-136`) on their own.

### Summary verdict

The ticket's "does a shape/precision/rounding change in `engine` silently cross into `orchestration`" concern doesn't materialize for this pair of packages — `rom/summary.py`'s runtime-validated `IndicatorSummary` and `cli/measure.py`'s narrow `MeasureResult`/`IndicatorOutcome` (boolean + string + `Path` only) fully absorb `engine`'s rich, untyped `CalculationResult.memoria` before anything reaches `orchestration`. The real, actionable finding is the reverse: `orchestration/run.py:162`'s direct, unguarded `load_manifest` call is the one place this package reaches past its own hardened adapter (`run_measure`) into config/engine-adjacent I/O without the try/except discipline every sibling call site at this boundary uses — it can crash the whole state machine on a bad `datasets.yaml`, and its `lru_cache` can silently serve a stale manifest across a `retry` decision.
